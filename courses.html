What I’m doing with my learning.

I’m not studying engineering formally, so I’m building this technical foundation on my own time through focused courses and certifications. I organise each one by primary skill area so the connection to my long-term wearable health-tech direction is clear.

Software Software courses and certifications give me structured technical depth I can apply in real builds. That foundation supports my long-term vision of building wearable systems that people can trust in real health decisions. Health Health-related learning keeps my technical direction aligned with patient reality. This directly supports my goal of creating tools that improve prevention and earlier intervention. Math Math coursework strengthens the analytical backbone behind decision-support systems. Those capabilities are critical to my vision because wearable health-tech must produce dependable insights, not just visual output. Health-Tech Health-tech certifications help me connect software and data with privacy, interoperability and patient usability standards. This is central to my long-term path toward wearable tools that make health data more accessible, understandable and useful.
